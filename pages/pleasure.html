Turning off any alarm is always associated with danger, with the caveat that there is simply no other way out in a given situation. The best example is anesthesia.

But that's in our natural world. How will it work in technology?

Partially suppressing alarms is possible if the entire situation is under some control. What does the situation itself mean? A situation is the presence of a point to which one must move from an existing position.

In first , we place the robot on starting point. All its sensors are screaming danger.It's impossible to move, as the role of the obstacles is unclear. Cameras and radars detect them but can't determine their nature. Can all these objects move? At what speed and how? How long will the fuel in the tank last if it's not already full? The possibility of this movement is studied through examples. Therefore, the first step is creating a database for movement. For simplicity, we will only discuss spatial movements here, but changes in state or execution of work can also be discussed.

Then we add a supervisor. The expert walks or drives along a section of the road, assessing it formally and through personal impressions. They fill in some text fields, but can also assess the situation involuntarily with head movements. If they're wearing augmented reality glasses, these emotions can be tracked as accompanying images of the road. This can also be supplemented with speech information. The driving experience may vary depending on the hazardous situations that arise during the trip, but in any case, this entire base can be described by various types of differential equations. This will make it possible to construct a mathematical model.

What is important here is that the various derivatives of this model correspond to the different levels of pleasure, that is, the disabled danger signals.
